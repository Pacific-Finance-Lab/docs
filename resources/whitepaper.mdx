The PAFI whitepaper sets out the protocol's design and the reasoning behind it.

## What the whitepaper carries

Why points are represented the way they are. Why a cycle has declared terms rather than negotiated ones. Why coverage is sized as it is and why an issuer stake deters rather than funds. The positions the protocol takes and the arguments for them.

## What these docs carry

What to do and what happens when you do it. Procedures, conditions, failure modes, parameters, and addresses.

The two do not restate each other. Where a docs page states a fact without arguing for it, the argument is in the whitepaper.
