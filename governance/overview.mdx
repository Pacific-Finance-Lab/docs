title: "Overview"
description: "What governance can and cannot do, and what it sets."
---

Governance is the owner of the protocol's contracts, controlled by PAFI stakers.

It decides listings and renewals, sets the global parameter defaults every listing is checked against, and holds the authority to upgrade the contracts.

## How a decision executes

Voting and execution are on-chain. A proposal that passes is queued and executes against the contracts after a delay.

Execution follows the recorded outcome rather than an assessment of it, so nothing sits between a passed vote and what happens.

## What governance sets

Global defaults set the bounds. A listing proposal may specify per-token values within them.

| Parameter | Governs |
|---|---|
| `CR_min` | The minimum coverage ratio a listing must meet |
| Minimum lock period | The shortest cycle a listing may declare |
| Staking minimum | The floor on an issuer's stake, and how it scales with declared budget |
| Liquidity placement template | The ranges within which opening liquidity may be placed |
| Grace period | How long after maturity before deprecation begins automatically |
| Holder exit window length | How long holders have to exchange against committed stablecoin |
| Timelock delay | How long a passed proposal is queued before execution |
| Fee rates | The swap protocol fee and the inventory sale fee |
| Supported quote assets | Which stablecoins may be used as quote assets |
| Lock-up tiers and fee discounts | What locking PAFI returns |

<Info>
  **Not yet set.** Current values are recorded on [Parameters](/developers/parameters).
</Info>

## When a parameter change takes effect

For cycles opened or renewed after the change.

A change in global defaults does not reach into an open cycle. An issuer that met `CR_min` at listing does not have to meet a higher one while that cycle is open, and a holder exposed to a cycle is exposed to the terms recorded when it opened.

## What governance cannot do

Three constraints are enforced in code rather than by policy. The terms recorded for a cycle cannot be altered while that cycle is open. Issuance in excess of the budget recorded for a cycle cannot be authorized. And an issuer's committed stablecoin cannot leave its position other than through the recorded paths.

These are properties of the contracts as deployed. Governance holds the authority to upgrade the contracts, so they describe what the deployed code does rather than a permanent limit on what governance could ever decide. The queue and delay before execution are the mechanism by which an upgrade becomes visible before it takes effect.

## Participation

Governance participation may be low, or concentrated among a small number of stakers. A listing decided by few voters executes on the same terms as one decided by many.
