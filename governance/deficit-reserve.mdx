---
title: "Deficit Reserve"
description: "When it can be applied and what it is not."
---

A defined portion of the Community and Ecosystem allocation is held as a Deficit Reserve, funded from that allocation and from protocol fee streams.

## Order of application

Where a deficit remains after the committed stablecoin and the issuer stake have been applied, governance may direct the reserve to it.

The reserve is third in order and is not reached until the two sources ahead of it are exhausted.

| Order | Source | Committed to a listing |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The issuer's committed stablecoin | Yes, at cycle open |
| 2 | The issuer's stake, where a slashing condition was met | Yes, while the listing is active |
| 3 | The Deficit Reserve | **No** |

## An affirmative vote

Applying the reserve requires an affirmative governance vote at the time.

The reserve is not applied automatically. No condition triggers it, no formula sizes it, and a prior application does not establish that a later one will occur.

## Limits

A governance-set amount rather than a committed one. It is not sized against any particular deficit and may be exhausted, in which case no further amount is available.

It confers no entitlement to payment and no right of recovery on any holder. A holder facing a shortfall has no claim on it, and a vote directing it to one deficit creates no expectation for the next.

It is not a guarantee of any kind, and nothing about its existence changes what the committed stablecoin covers or what a holder exit window pays from.

## The decision

Whether to direct a limited pool of ecosystem funds at a specific shortfall, knowing that doing so reduces what is available for the next one.

An issuer failing on recorded terms is a different case from a market moving. The two sources ahead of the reserve must be exhausted before it is reached. The reserve is finite and is not replenished on any schedule. And a prior application does not bind a later vote, though it does shape expectations.
