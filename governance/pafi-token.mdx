## The four functions

| | Function | |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Governance** | Listing, renewal, and parameter decisions are made by PAFI stakers |
| 2 | **Issuer staking** | An issuer stakes PAFI as a condition of submitting a listing proposal, in an amount determined with reference to its declared issuance budget |
| 3 | **Fee benefits** | Fee discounts on protocol transactions through token lock-up |
| 4 | **Ecosystem incentives** | Quarterly distribution to integrated issuers by transaction volume |

Governance and issuer staking are the load-bearing functions.

## Staking

Staking PAFI is what makes an account a voter. An account that does not hold stake does not vote.

Staked PAFI is not slashable in connection with a listing outcome, and it is not a source of funds for a holder exit window. What is at risk in connection with a listing is the reward recorded against the proposal, described in [Vote rewards](/governance/vote-rewards).

## Lock-up

Locking PAFI discounts protocol transaction fees.

<Info>
  **Not yet set.** Lock-up tiers and the discounts attached to them are recorded on [Parameters](/developers/parameters).
</Info>

## Ecosystem incentives

A quarterly distribution to integrated issuers, by transaction volume.

## Voter stake and issuer stake

Two separate mechanisms with separate subjects.

| | Voter stake | Issuer stake |
|---|---|---|
| Held by | Any account that votes | The issuer submitting a proposal |
| Sized by | The account | A governance-set relation to declared issuance budget |
| Reducible | No | On two enumerated procedural conditions |
| Funds a holder exit window | No | No |

A holder exit window is funded from the issuer's committed stablecoin, exclusively.

The two enumerated conditions and where a slashed stake goes are in [Terms and commitments](/issuers/listing/terms-and-commitments).

## What the token is not

PAFI is a utility token. Its functions are limited to the four above.

It confers no equity, no ownership, no debt, and no right to profits, dividends, revenues, or returns of any kind.

## Supply and allocation

Total supply is **1,000,000,000 PAFI**.

| Category | Allocation | Cliff | Vesting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Community and Ecosystem | 65% (650,000,000) | None | Declining emission |
| Team and Advisors | 15% (150,000,000) | 12 months | 48 months total |
| Contributors | 8% (80,000,000) | 12 months | 36 months |
| Liquidity | 8% (80,000,000) | None | Unlocked at TGE |
| Strategic Ecosystem | 4% (40,000,000) | 18 months | 45 months total |

The Liquidity allocation is deployed for exchange listings and initial liquidity provisioning.

## Emission

Up to 15% of total supply is unlocked at TGE from the Community and Ecosystem allocation, on predefined milestones, followed by a declining schedule.

Approximately 150,000,000 tokens are distributed at TGE, then approximately 26,000,000 in the first year, 13,000,000 in the second, and 6,500,000 in the third.

The Deficit Reserve is held from this same allocation. See [Deficit Reserve](/governance/deficit-reserve).
