---
title: "Fees"
description: "What trading on the Gateway costs and who collects it."
---

Four things can cost money. Only two of them are protocol fees.

| | What it is | Paid to |
|---|---|---|
| **Swap protocol fee** | Taken on volume in the Gateway's pools | The protocol |
| **Inventory sale fee** | Taken on stablecoin paid out to an issuer above its committed amount | The protocol |
| **Price impact** | The gap between a pool's current price and the price an order fills at | Nobody |
| **Venue fees** | Whatever an integrated venue charges | That venue |

The two protocol fees are set by governance.

## Swap protocol fee

Applies to volume in the Gateway's pools, whether converting to a stablecoin or trading between assets.

Locking PAFI discounts protocol transaction fees. See [PAFI token](/governance/pafi-token).

## Inventory sale fee

Applies where an issuer withdraws stablecoin above its committed amount, either during a cycle or as a residual at the close of deprecation.

**It is not applied to the committed amount itself,** so an issuer's principal is not reduced by protocol fees.

## Price impact is not a fee

Nobody collects it. It is what happens when an order is large relative to the pool, and it is why the same trade costs more on a thin pool than a deep one.

The screen shows it before confirmation.

## Network fees

A holder does not need to hold a gas token. The network fee for an action is settled in the asset that action already involves.

## Venue fees

An integrated venue sets its own fees and charges them there.

Where a holder routes through PAFI's integrations, the protocol may receive a share of that venue's trading fee under the venue's builder or referral arrangements. That share is determined by the venue and does not add to what the holder pays.

## No fee

Converting points into point tokens. Redeeming point tokens for points. Neither is charged by the protocol.

No listing fee or cycle fee applies to an issuer.
