title: "Who operates what"
description: "The five parties and what each operates."
---

Five parties act along the path in [How PAFI works](/learn/how-pafi-works). Each acts on its own account.

| Party | Operates |
|---|---|
| **The issuer** | Its own point system, ledger, eligibility policy, and off-chain redemption terms. What a point is, what it is worth off-chain, and whether it can be returned for off-chain goods, services, or rewards |
| **PAFI** | The Gateway's spot exchange for point token conversion, together with the routing, data, and settlement infrastructure that connects the resulting liquidity to integrated venues |
| **PAFI stakers, through governance** | Listing and renewal decisions, global parameter defaults, and the authority to upgrade the contracts |
| **An integrated venue** | Its own markets, under its own licenses and registrations. Margin accounts, order matching, funding, and liquidation sit there, under its own risk engine |
| **The holder** | The decision to convert, to exchange, to hold, to withdraw, or to route, and the choice of path for each |

PAFI functions as gateway and liquidity-routing infrastructure. It is not an issuer, a custodian, or a guarantor of any point or of any asset represented on the Gateway. It does not operate, and does not hold a license for, leveraged perpetual, margin, or securities derivatives trading. It does not set or enforce an issuer's off-chain redemption or utilization policy. Beyond the infrastructure described above it does not intermediate between an issuer and a holder, or between a holder and an integrated venue, and it does not guarantee listing, execution terms, or liquidity at any venue. The PAFI Agent provides neither investment advice nor discretionary asset management.

The PAFI protocol is non-custodial infrastructure. Responsibility for the terms an issuer declares, and for the consequences of those terms, rests with the issuer and with individual market participants.
