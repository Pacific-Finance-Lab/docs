## Two domains

The off-chain domain is the issuer's. Its applications, its ledger, its eligibility policy, and its redemption terms sit there and stay there.

The on-chain domain holds the terms recorded for a cycle, the pools, and the routing layer.

## The four components

| Component | What it is |
|---|---|
| **PAFI Gateway** | A permissionless, issuer-gated on-chain spot exchange in which point tokens are exchanged against a USD stablecoin or against PAFI |
| **Routing layer** | The layer connecting liquidity realized on the Gateway to integrated venues |
| **Access layer** | The accounts and interfaces through which holders reach the Gateway |
| **Integration interfaces** | The interfaces through which an issuer connects a point system, and through which applications offer conversion, trading, or routing to their own users |

## Who decides what

An issuer sets its own programme and declares the terms on which its point token trades. PAFI stakers decide whether those terms open a market. PAFI operates the Gateway and the routing layer. Integrated venues operate their own markets.

Enumerated in [Who operates what](/learn/who-operates-what).

## What is enforced in code

1. The terms recorded for a cycle cannot be altered while that cycle is open.
2. Issuance in excess of the budget recorded for a cycle cannot be authorized.
3. An issuer's committed stablecoin cannot leave its position other than through the recorded paths.

See [Governance](/governance/overview) for what can still be changed and how.
