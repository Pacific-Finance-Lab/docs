---
title: "Listing cycles"
description: "How a listing reaches a market and what happens at maturity."
---

A cycle is the period for which a set of declared terms governs a point token's market.

A market does not open because an issuer connects a point system. It opens because that issuer declared terms, those terms met the listing criteria, and PAFI stakers approved them.

## From proposal to market

An issuer stakes PAFI first. A proposal from an issuer that has not met the staking requirement does not proceed.

It then submits a listing proposal declaring its terms. The proposal is checked against the listing criteria before any vote takes place, and one that does not meet them does not reach a vote. PAFI stakers decide the proposals that do.

On approval the contracts commit and lock the issuer's stablecoin and open the pools on the declared terms. The cycle runs, and the recorded terms are not modifiable while it does.

At maturity the cycle renews on terms approved in advance, or enters deprecation.

## Declared terms

An opening price. An amount of USD stablecoin it deposits and locks for the cycle. A limit on how many point tokens can be outstanding. How long the stablecoin stays locked. And how quickly it undertakes to credit points when a holder redeems.

## Listing criteria

The committed stablecoin must cover the issuance limit valued at the opening price, by a governance-set margin. The lock period must be at least a governance-set minimum. The issuer must have met the staking requirement. And opening liquidity must be placed within a standard template.

A proposal that reaches a vote already meets all four. What voters decide is the issuer and its terms within those bounds.

The terms, the relation between them, and how to choose them are in [Terms and commitments](/issuers/listing/terms-and-commitments). The governance-set values are listed in [Governance](/governance/overview).

## Deprecation

Deprecation runs as a defined contract sequence in this order. Issuance stops. Withdrawals of the excess above the committed amount freeze. A holder exit window opens, during which point tokens may be exchanged against the committed stablecoin. Only after that window closes may the issuer withdraw any residual.

If no approved renewal exists at maturity, deprecation begins automatically following a grace period.

There is no state in which the lock period has expired and the timing of withdrawal is indeterminate.

See [Risks](/learn/risks) and [Running and ending a cycle](/issuers/running/running-and-ending-a-cycle).

## The limits of a cycle

The quantity and the sequence of an issuer's actions.

It does not bound the commercial value of its points. An issuer that meets every recorded term may still reduce what its points are worth by changing prices, catalogues, or redemption terms within its own systems, and those decisions sit outside the recorded terms.
