A point balance passes through five states between being earned and leaving the system.

<Note>
  **[D5] Diagram.** Lifecycle. Five states with the transitions between them. This is the page other sections point back to for context.
</Note>

## Point tokens

A transferable on-chain representation of an issuer-defined point balance.

Its value comes from issuer-defined utility and nothing further. Representing points on-chain does not alter what the issuer accepts the underlying points for, and PAFI does not standardize or enforce point utility, redemption, or off-chain policy.

Each listing deploys its own token contract, so an issuer running more than one programme can list more than one point token. Point tokens are not interchangeable with one another.

## Earned

A consumer earns points in the issuer's application. The balance is credited in the issuer's own ledger.

Nothing is on-chain yet, and nothing about this state changes when a programme lists.

## Converted

The holder requests conversion. The issuer's own systems evaluate the request against its own eligibility policy, deduct the points, and issue point tokens to the holder's account.

The issuer submits this. The holder signs nothing.

## Held and exchanged

The point token sits in the holder's account. It can be held, transferred, or exchanged in the Gateway's pools against a USD stablecoin or against PAFI.

Price here is a market outcome rather than a redemption value, and it can sit above or below what the same points are worth inside the issuer's systems.

## Returned, or moved on

Three exits, each depending on a different condition.

| Exit | Depends on |
|---|---|
| Redeem for off-chain points | The issuer's integration being active |
| Exchange in the pool | Pool liquidity |
| Exchange against the issuer's committed stablecoin | A holder exit window being open |

They do not fail together, which is why there are three.

| Situation | Path |
|---|---|
| The holder wants to keep using the issuer's programme | Redeem |
| The holder wants stable value now, and the pool has depth | Exchange in the pool |
| The issuer has stopped processing redemptions | Exchange in the pool |
| The listing is in deprecation | Exit window, or exchange |

Stablecoin realized in the second and third can be held, withdrawn, or routed to an integrated venue.

## End of cycle

At maturity the cycle renews on terms approved in advance, or enters deprecation.

Renewal continues everything above. Deprecation stops issuance, opens the holder exit window, and only then releases any residual to the issuer.

See [Listing cycles](/learn/listing-cycles).

## How much can be converted

A listing carries an issuance limit and conversion stops when outstanding supply reaches it. Redeeming frees the limit again, so a programme where holders both convert and redeem does not exhaust it through ordinary circulation.

The limit and how it is set are in [Terms and commitments](/issuers/listing/terms-and-commitments).
