You can create an account from an issuer's app or from the PAFI web app. Either route creates the same account.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the sign-in">
    From an issuer's app, select the option to connect a PAFI account. From the PAFI web app, sign in directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Enter the code sent to your address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    If you started from an issuer's app, the app receives your account address and you return to it.

    <Note>
      **\[S1\] Screenshot.** The verification screen.

      <Frame>
        ![Image](/images/image.png "Image")
      </Frame>
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## One account across everything

The account you reach from an issuer's app and the account you reach from the PAFI web app are the same account, holding the same balances.

Signing in from a second issuer's app does not create a second account. Point tokens from every issuer you have converted with sit in one place.

Use the same email address everywhere. An issuer's app sends the address it has on file, and if that address differs from the one you sign in with, the connection stops rather than creating a second account.

## If you already have a wallet

Connect it instead. No email step applies.

## Network fees

You do not need a gas token. The network fee for an action is settled in the asset that action already involves.

## Checkpoint

Your account address and your point balance appear together.

<Note>
  **Session.** An active session can survive closing the app. Sign-in appears again on a new device, after the session expires, or after you sign out.
</Note>
