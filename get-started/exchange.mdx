## Price

Supply and demand within the pool. The Gateway does not set or bound prices.

A price you see is not a redemption value. It can sit above or below what the same points are worth inside the issuer's own systems, in either direction and for extended periods.

Neither the Gateway nor the issuer is obliged to buy at any price.

## Routes

The direct stablecoin pair is the primary route and is maintained for every point token. The router takes a PAFI pair instead where it finds that path deeper.

A path through a PAFI pair carries the price movement of PAFI in addition to that of the point token. The direct pair stays available.

## Supplying liquidity

Pools accept liquidity from anyone. A liquidity position changes composition as the price moves and is not the same as holding the asset.

## Checkpoint

Stablecoin is in your account.
