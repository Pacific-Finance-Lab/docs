Routing moves stablecoin you realized on the Gateway into a market operated somewhere else.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a destination">
    Destinations include perpetual exchanges, centralized exchanges, neobanking rails, and applications that settle in stablecoins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the route">
    The screen returns the resolved parameters and the expected cost before anything is submitted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Where the terms change materially between review and confirmation, the action returns for confirmation again.

    You do not open a separate account relationship with the destination, and you do not acquire that venue's native gas asset first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## At the destination

Margin accounts, order matching, funding, and liquidation are the destination venue's, under its own risk engine. Its listings, leverage parameters, and regional eligibility are set there.

PAFI operates the conversion market and the routing layer. See [Who operates what](/learn/who-operates-what).

## Other options

Holding the stablecoin, withdrawing it, and deploying it elsewhere are all available. No destination is privileged by the protocol.

## Price-referenced markets

Commodity and equity markets reached this way are price-referenced. Exposure is to price movement and carries no delivery, no ownership, no voting right, and no dividend.

<Note>
  A price-referenced perpetual can diverge from the market it tracks, and outside that market's trading hours it reflects expected movement rather than executed trades.
</Note>

## Checkpoint

The position appears at the destination venue and is managed there.
