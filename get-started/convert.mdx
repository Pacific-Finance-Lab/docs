title: "Convert points to a point token"
description: "Turn a point balance in an issuer ledger into a point token."
---

Conversion turns a point balance in an issuer's ledger into a point token in your account.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request conversion in the issuer's app">
    Enter the amount. Conversion starts in the app, not on the Gateway.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The issuer checks eligibility">
    Whether a balance is eligible, and in what amount, is the issuer's decision under its own policy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The issuer submits the issuance">
    Points are deducted and point tokens are created in your account. You do not sign anything for this step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tokens appear">
    Issuance takes a few seconds, longer when the network is congested.

    <Note>
      **[S2] Screenshot.** The conversion screen in an issuer's app. Blocked on UI.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Why a conversion can fail

| Reason | What it means |
|---|---|
| Ineligible under the issuer's policy | The issuer declined. Its policy, not PAFI's |
| Issuance budget reached | The cycle has an issuance limit and outstanding supply is at it. Redemptions restore budget |
| Transaction failed | Resubmit. Points are not deducted until it succeeds |

## Going back

Redemption returns tokens to points. It burns the tokens and credits points in the issuer's ledger, and needs both the issuer's approval and yours.

See [The life of a point](/learn/life-of-a-point).

## Checkpoint

The token balance in your account matches the amount deducted from your point balance.
