---

Listing a point programme takes four things from you and leaves three things with you.

Read [The life of a point](/learn/life-of-a-point) first if you have not. This section assumes it.

## What you need

A point programme with its own ledger, since balances stay in your systems. PAFI to stake, which is a precondition for submitting a proposal. USD stablecoin to commit, deposited at cycle open and locked for the period you declare. And the engineering capacity to build six backend endpoints, one event subscription, and two buttons.

## What stays yours

What a point is. What it is worth off-chain. Whether and on what terms it can be returned for goods, services, or rewards. Who is eligible to convert, and in what amount.

None of these are recorded terms. You can change them during a cycle.

## What you take on

Your committed stablecoin is locked and cannot be withdrawn during the lock period. Outstanding supply cannot exceed the budget you declare. Your stake is slashable on two enumerated conditions and nothing else. And your redemption processing time becomes a recorded term, which is the only operational commitment carrying a slashing condition.

## Who decides

PAFI stakers, through governance.

A proposal is checked against the listing criteria before any vote takes place, and one that does not meet them does not reach a vote. What stakers then decide is you and your terms within those bounds.

PAFI does not approve listings and cannot guarantee one.

## What a market gives you

An observable price for a balance you previously priced only internally, and a route through which holders realize value without waiting for a use you offer.

It does not oblige you to buy at any price and it does not set a floor.

## Where to start

Choose your terms in [Terms and commitments](/issuers/listing/terms-and-commitments), submit through [Submitting a proposal](/issuers/listing/submitting-a-proposal), and build from [How the integration works](/issuers/integration/how-it-works).

The integration does not depend on approval. Build and test it before a vote so you can issue on the day your pools open.
