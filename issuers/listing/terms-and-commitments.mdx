A listing proposal declares six terms. This page covers each one, what choosing it does, and what it commits you to.

## The six terms

| Term | Symbol | Sets |
|---|---|---|
| Initial reference price | `P0` | Where your pools open |
| Committed USD stablecoin | `I` | What you deposit and lock |
| User conversion allocation | `U` | How much holders can convert into |
| Issuer inventory allocation | `L` | How much you issue to yourself |
| Lock period | | How long `I` stays locked |
| Redemption processing commitment | | How long you have to credit points |

`U + L` is your issuance budget. Outstanding supply cannot exceed it.

## `P0`

An opening parameter. It sets where your pools open and it is the price the coverage relation is measured against.

It is not a floor and it is not a redemption value. Once the pools open, price is a market outcome and can sit above or below `P0` in either direction and for extended periods.

Setting `P0` high raises the stablecoin you must commit for the same budget.

## `U` and `L`

`U` is what your holders convert into. Size it against how much of your outstanding point liability you expect converted during the cycle, not against the whole liability.

`L` is what you issue to yourself and supply to the pool as liquidity. This is your opening depth. A thin pool means holders exiting incur slippage and a large sale moves your price materially.

Both count against the same budget and both are measured by the coverage relation, so raising `L` raises what you must commit.

You can raise `U` during the cycle while the coverage relation continues to hold. You cannot raise `L` during the cycle.

## `I`

`I` must satisfy the coverage relation.

```
I  >=  CR_min x (U + L) x P0
```

<Info>
  **Not yet set.** `CR_min` is recorded on [Parameters](/developers/parameters).
</Info>

Two things about `I` matter more than the number.

**It is sized at `P0` and does not track price.** If outstanding supply is at its limit and the exit price is above `P0`, `I` may be insufficient to satisfy every holder at that price.

**It is the only source of funds for a holder exit window.** Your stake is not. The Deficit Reserve is not.

`I` is deposited at cycle open and locked by contract for the lock period. Anything you hold above it is withdrawable, which is how you take the proceeds of inventory sales during a cycle. An inventory sale fee applies to stablecoin paid out above the committed amount and is not applied to the committed amount itself, so protocol fees do not reduce your principal.

Withdrawals of the excess freeze once deprecation begins. At the close of deprecation, what remains after the holder exit window is your residual.

## Lock period

At least a governance-set minimum. Longer is permitted.

This is the period a holder is exposed to, and the period during which none of your declared terms can be altered, including by you.

Size `I` against capital you can commit for the full period. There is no early exit.

<Info>
  **Not yet set.** The minimum lock period is recorded on [Parameters](/developers/parameters).
</Info>

## Redemption processing commitment

The time within which you credit points once a holder redeems a point token.

**This is the one declared term carrying a slashing condition.** Set it to a time your operations can meet on your worst day, not your average one.

It covers processing time only. What the points are worth once credited stays yours.

## Your stake

Staked before you submit. Not a declared term, a precondition. A proposal from an issuer that has not met the requirement does not proceed.

The required amount is governance-set and scales with the issuance budget you declare.

<Info>
  **Not yet set.** The protocol-wide minimum and the relation between budget and required stake are recorded on [Parameters](/developers/parameters).
</Info>

<Info>
  **To confirm.** Whether the requirement is assessed per listing or across an issuer's listings in aggregate.
</Info>

Your stake stays committed while the listing is active and is releasable after the deprecation sequence completes.

## The two slashing conditions

Enumerated in the contracts. There are two, and there is no authority to slash outside them.

| Condition | |
|---|---|
| **Incomplete deprecation** | Proceeding to withdraw without completing the deprecation sequence |
| **Redemption commitment breach** | Breach of the processing time you recorded |

Both turn on a recorded procedure or a recorded term.

**Nothing turns on an assessment of your conduct.** Your stake is not reduced because your programme performed poorly, because your points lost value, because a holder was dissatisfied, or because a listing was judged in hindsight to have been unwise.

A slashed stake goes to the liquidity contract of the point token concerned, with any remainder to the treasury. That transfer changes the contract's balance and does not create a claim, an entitlement, or a right of recovery for any individual holder.

## Pairs and liquidity placement

Every point token has a USD stablecoin pair, created for you.

You may additionally declare a PAFI pair, which makes liquidity providers already holding PAFI able to supply your market on the day it opens.

Your opening liquidity must be placed within the ranges a standard template permits. Within that template you choose the distribution.

<Info>
  **Not yet set.** The template is recorded on [Parameters](/developers/parameters).
</Info>

## During a cycle

| Can | Condition |
|---|---|
| Deposit additional stablecoin | Any time |
| Raise `U` | While the coverage relation continues to hold |
| Withdraw stablecoin above the committed amount | Until deprecation begins |

| Cannot | |
|---|---|
| Withdraw `I` | During the lock period |
| Issue beyond `U + L` | |
| Raise `L` | |
| Alter any recorded term | |
| Withdraw the excess | Once deprecation has begun |
| Take a residual | Before the holder exit window has closed |
