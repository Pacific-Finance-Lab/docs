## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Stake PAFI">
    The required amount is determined with reference to the issuance budget you intend to declare.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the proposal">
    Declare the six terms from [Terms and commitments](/issuers/listing/terms-and-commitments).
  </Step>
  <Step title="The criteria are checked">
    Coverage, lock period, staking, and the liquidity placement template are checked before any vote takes place. A proposal that does not meet them does not reach a vote.
  </Step>
  <Step title="PAFI stakers vote">
    Voting and execution are on-chain. A proposal that passes is queued and executes against the contracts after a delay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The cycle opens">
    Your stablecoin is committed and locked, your terms are recorded, and your pools open at `P0` with your inventory allocation supplied as liquidity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Scope of the vote

You, and your terms within the checked bounds.

The criteria constrain the terms of a listing. They do not assess your business, your solvency, or the durability of your programme. That assessment is what the vote adds.

## If a proposal does not pass

Your stake is not slashed. Slashing conditions attach to an active listing, and neither of the two is a failed proposal.

Revise the terms and submit again.

## Multiple programmes

Each listing deploys its own token contract with its own cycle, budget, and committed stablecoin. If you run more than one programme you can list more than one point token.

## Renewal

Renewal proposals go through the same route and are decided by the same voters.

**Renewal must be approved before maturity.** An unapproved renewal does not hold a cycle open, and deprecation begins automatically after a grace period. Submit with enough time for a vote and the timelock delay.

See [Running and ending a cycle](/issuers/running/running-and-ending-a-cycle).
